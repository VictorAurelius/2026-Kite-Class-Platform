# PDF Cover Styles — Catalog

Adapted from the MiniMax `minimax-pdf` skill. Pick a style that matches the document's role (official vs marketing vs editorial) and the tenant's brand tone.

| # | Style | Use when | Typography cues |
|:-:|-------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Classic Centered** | Tax invoices, legal contracts, transcripts | Large centered title, subtitle in small caps, single rule line below |
| 2 | **Left-Aligned Minimal** | Internal reports, dashboards-to-PDF | Title flush left, metadata table, generous whitespace |
| 3 | **Full-Bleed Hero** | Marketing pitch, brand deck | Edge-to-edge tinted header, large white title, brand accent bar |
| 4 | **Official Government** | Hóa đơn GTGT, tax receipts, compliance letters | "CỘNG HÒA XÃ HỘI CHỦ NGHĨA VIỆT NAM" header, centered title in caps |
| 5 | **Letterhead + Logo Left** | Admission letters, welcome packs | Logo top-left, address block top-right, title 1/3 down |
| 6 | **Two-Column Masthead** | Newsletters, catalog covers | Date strip + title on one side, hero image on other |
| 7 | **Certificate Ornate** | Course completion, achievement awards | Double-line border, calligraphic title, centered recipient name |
| 8 | **Stamp + Seal** | Notarized forms, school seals | Reserved stamp area bottom-right, title top-center |
| 9 | **Report Cover Plain** | Financial statements, board reports | Title block on tinted band, report period + entity beneath |
| 10 | **Side-Bar Color** | Course syllabi, handbooks | 2–3cm vertical accent bar in brand color, title offset right |
| 11 | **Gridded Data Cover** | Annual/quarterly summaries | 2×2 KPI tile layout above title (large numbers + labels) |
| 12 | **Tri-Fold Panel** | Printed brochures exported to PDF | 3-column layout, fold marks drawn faintly |
| 13 | **Hero Photo + Overlay** | Events, conferences, recaps | Full-width image with tinted overlay, title in serif display |
| 14 | **Editorial Magazine** | Long-form reports, "yearbook" outputs | Drop cap, pull quote, section rule lines |
| 15 | **Minimal Mark** | Release notes, changelogs | Brand mark top-center only, title on single line, aggressively minimal |

**Wave 5 scope:** only style #4 (Official Government) ships with the invoice template. File GAP-208 to expand.
