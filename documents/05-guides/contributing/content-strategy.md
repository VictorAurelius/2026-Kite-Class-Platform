# KiteHub Content Strategy — Q2 2026 Editorial Plan

**Audience:** owners / directors of Vietnamese education centers (English, IELTS, coding, tutoring) evaluating SaaS platforms.
**Primary goal:** drive organic SEO traffic into `/pricing` and `/register`.
**Cadence:** 1 post/week × 12 weeks = 12 posts (April → June 2026).

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Vietnamese-first** — primary language `vi`; English translations defer to Wave 10+.
2. **Educator pain-point → KiteHub solution** — every post links to at least one product surface (pricing tier, feature page, trial signup).
3. **E-E-A-T signals** — author bylines with role + credentials; cite MOET circulars, Ministry of Labor regulations where relevant.
4. **No AI slop** — drafts may use AI but every post reviewed by a human owner listed above before publish.
5. **Gate via `marketing-legal-review` skill** (GAP-174) — brand + legal + claims compliance before merge.

## 4. Post Production Checklist

Every post PR must tick:

- [ ] Primary keyword in title + H1 + first 100 words
- [ ] 3+ H2 subheadings with secondary keywords
- [ ] Word count 1,200–2,500 (sweet spot for VN commercial intent)
- [ ] Internal links: ≥2 to product pages (`/pricing`, `/register`, `/blog/*`)
- [ ] External links: 1–2 authoritative (MOET, General Statistics Office, reputable VN edu media)
- [ ] 1 hero image (`public/blog/{slug}/hero.png`, 1200×630, OG-ready)
- [ ] Author + date + tags populated in MDX frontmatter
- [ ] Reviewed per `marketing-legal-review` skill (GAP-174)
- [ ] `generateMetadata` exposes OG + Twitter + canonical automatically (existing pipeline)
- [ ] Lighthouse SEO ≥ 90 on the published URL (Lighthouse CI)

---

## 5. Review Workflow (ties to GAP-174)

```
1. Draft PR opened (author + date + tags in frontmatter)
2. Marketing reviewer runs `marketing-legal-review` skill — blocks merge if:
   - Claims unsubstantiated ("giảm 80% thời gian" — needs data source or softer copy)
   - Competitor names used without neutral framing
   - Pricing or guarantee claims inconsistent with /pricing page
3. Legal spot-check (async, 24h SLA) — required only for posts touching:
   - Compliance (MOET, labor law, PDPL)
   - Payments / refund claims
   - Data handling / student privacy
4. Editor merges; CI deploys preview; Lighthouse CI gate.
5. Post-publish: Google Search Console submission within 24h; monitor CTR at 30-day mark.
```
