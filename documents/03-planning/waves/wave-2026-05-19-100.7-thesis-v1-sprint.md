### Phase 3 — Bibliography + DOCX pipeline (~1.5h)

13. /clear breakpoint after Phase 3 merge

### Phase 4 — Coordinator review + ship V1 (~45 min)

14. Coordinator read `thesis-v1.docx` sample pages (TOC + Chapter 1 intro + Chapter 4 intro)
